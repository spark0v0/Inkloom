Use case: ui-mockup. Create a high fidelity flat, crisp design concept board for Inkloom, a Chinese long-form serial fiction writing application. Wide high resolution composition, two desktop screens side by side, the writing workspace taking two thirds of the board and library one third. A quiet, exceptionally polished editorial tool for hours of reading. No perspective, devices, browser frame, illustrations or marketing banners. Low-chroma pale sage-grey outer frame #f1f4f2, white inset sheet, fine neutral hairlines, charcoal #242b28, secondary grey #626c66 and deep teal #235b4b on one main action and small selected marks. Chinese sans-serif UI, relaxed readable manuscript. No gradients, glass, shadows, bright coloured tiles, huge titles, fake metrics, avatars or charts.
Writing workspace: fixed labelled primary navigation about 180px on far left. Inkloom and small pen nib outline, current book 雾港来信, destinations 我的作品、大纲与章节、章节写作、人物与设定、连载导出; settings and backup at bottom. A 48px compact breadcrumb bar above the inset work surface. Compact toolbar with 正文 / AI 草稿 tabs, bold italic undo, font selectors and collapse controls.
